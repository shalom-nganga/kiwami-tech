FAQ

Frequently asked
questions.

Quick answers about software testing, ISTQB certification, and our services.

Software testing is a process of evaluating a software application to ensure it meets specified requirements and functions correctly. It involves running test cases, identifying defects, and ensuring the quality and reliability of the software.
Software testing is crucial for ensuring quality, reliability, and performance. It helps identify defects early in the development process, reduces the risk of costly errors in production, and enhances user satisfaction.
ISTQB certification is a globally recognised program that validates a tester's knowledge and proficiency in software testing principles, methodologies, and best practices.
Kiwami School offers ISTQB certification preparation, manual testing, automation testing (Playwright, Selenium), USSD testing, API testing, AWS Practitioner prep, and more — for all levels.
Simply reach out through our contact form, email, or WhatsApp. We'll assess your needs and develop a customised testing solution for your project.
We specialise in USSD and mobile money testing — unique disciplines critical to East African fintech. Combined with ISTQB-certified trainers and 15+ years of experience, we're the QA partner built for this market.
