1. Analyse the Project Requirements

We start by thoroughly analyzing the project requirements to gain a comprehensive understanding of your goals and objectives.

2. Strategize the Testing Approach to Use

Based on the project requirements, we strategize the most effective testing approach to ensure thorough coverage and optimal results.

3. Generate the Test Plan Document

We create a detailed test plan document outlining the testing strategy, scope, objectives, and methodologies to be employed throughout the project lifecycle.

4. Allocate Dedicated Resources for the Project

We allocate dedicated resources, including skilled professionals and necessary tools, to ensure the successful execution of the project within the defined timelines and budget.

5. Test Execution and Daily Reporting

Our team executes the test plan meticulously, conducting tests, identifying issues, and providing daily reporting to keep you informed about the progress and any potential challenges.

6. Launch and Set-Up of CI/CD Operations

Upon completion of testing, we assist in the seamless launch and set-up of CI/CD operations to ensure efficient deployment and delivery of your software products.

7. Monitoring and Analysis

We continuously monitor the performance and behavior of your software products post-launch, analyzing data and gathering insights to identify areas for improvement.
